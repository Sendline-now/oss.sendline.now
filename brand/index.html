Library marks

Each mark is the Sendline patch in the library's colour, with the library's glyph knocked out in a dark ink of the same hue — the colour-and-ink pair sendline.now gives every section. The wordmarks are drawn with the SENDLINE letters' construction: one stroke of 7, square caps, a 42-unit advance, and the two-part rule, broken where the name meets the shared 4S. No fonts and no images inside any file.
